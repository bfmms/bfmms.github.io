About us

Law Firm BANOVIĆ, FOSIĆ, MARGIĆ and STANIČIĆ Ltd. (abbreviated as BFMS) provides comprehensive legal services to companies and individuals in the Republic of Croatia and abroad. With long-standing experience and a commitment to professional diligence, our team is dedicated to protecting clients’ interests according to high professional standards.

Filip Banović

Attorney-at-law

An expert in corporate law, contractual relations and litigation, with the longest tenure within the team, contributing to stability and a high level of legal expertise. Over the years of practice, he has become a reliable partner in resolving commercial disputes and employment-law challenges, with a particular focus on precisely drafted contracts that prevent legal risks. His courtroom and negotiation experience makes him a key support for domestic and foreign clients.

Danijel Fosić

Attorney-at-law

Expert in public procurement law and labour law. He completed a postgraduate programme in public procurement law at the University of Rome Tor Vergata. As an authorised trainer and lecturer in the field of public procurement law, and as a sworn court interpreter for the English language, he conducts formal training and professional development programmes in public procurement law and provides legal support to clients in complex national and international proceedings.

Mislav Margić

Attorney-at-law

An expert in contract law and real estate law, with unique professional experience gained through many years of work in the public sector on enforcement and real estate matters. He provides clients with in-depth insight into institutional processes and a high level of legal certainty in transactions, offering comprehensive support in complex legal matters with an international element.

Tin Matija Staničić

Attorney-at-law

An expert whose work includes advising on real estate transactions, contractual relations and employment-law matters, with extensive experience in resolving property-law disputes. As a permanent court interpreter for English and German, he is a key support to foreign clients in aligning their business operations with the Croatian legal framework.

Practice areas

Public Procurement

Advice in public procurement procedures, preparation of bids, drafting of appeals and representation before DKOM.

Commercial Law

Company formation, status changes, mergers and acquisitions, corporate governance and compliance.

Real Estate

Sale and purchase, lease, project development, due diligence, land registry registrations and all aspects of real estate law.

Litigation

Representation before all courts in civil, commercial and administrative disputes, as well as arbitration proceedings.

Employment Law

Employment contracts, collective agreements, restructurings, employment disputes and advice on all employment law matters.

Contract Law

Drafting, negotiating and reviewing all types of commercial contracts, including international transactions.
