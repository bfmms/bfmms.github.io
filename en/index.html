Law firm in Zagreb, Croatia

Law Firm Banović, Fosić, Margić and Staničić Ltd.

Law Firm Banović, Fosić, Margić and Staničić Ltd. is based in Zagreb and provides legal services to domestic and foreign clients in business, property, employment and procedural matters. The firm supports companies, contracting authorities, tenderers in public procurement procedures, individuals, investors and other clients who require legal analysis, document preparation, negotiations or representation before competent authorities and courts in Croatia.

The firm’s practice covers public procurement, corporate and commercial law, real estate, employment law, litigation and enforcement, as well as contract law. In each matter the focus is on legal precision, the commercial and factual context of the case and clear explanation of the legal consequences that may follow from a transaction, procedure, contract or dispute.

Practice areas

Legal services provided by BFMS Law Firm

The following areas describe the legal work in which the firm provides advice, document drafting, procedural support and representation.

Public procurement

BFMS provides legal support in Croatian public procurement procedures, including advice to tenderers and contracting authorities, review of procurement documents, preparation of clarification requests, analysis of selection criteria, technical specifications and requirements relating to professional and technical capacity. Support also includes drafting appeals to the Croatian State Commission for Supervision of Public Procurement Procedures, responses to appeals, analysis of award and cancellation decisions and representation in related administrative disputes. Public procurement matters require timely assessment of procedural deadlines, evidence and legal risks that may affect participation in the procedure. The work combines knowledge of procurement legislation, the practice of the competent review bodies and the market context in which economic operators prepare and submit tenders.

Corporate law and business advisory

In corporate and commercial law, BFMS advises companies, shareholders, management boards and business partners on incorporation, corporate changes, internal relations, business documentation and contractual relations. Legal support includes drafting and reviewing articles of association, shareholders’ decisions, intercompany agreements, business cooperation agreements, general terms of business and other documents used in daily operations. Particular attention is given to legal certainty, clarity of obligations and predictable consequences in the event of non-performance, termination or dispute. In business advisory work, legal analysis is connected with the practical needs of the client, especially in contract negotiations, risk management, collection of receivables and resolution of disputes between business entities.

Real estate and land registry matters

BFMS provides legal services related to real estate in Croatia, including sale and purchase, lease, tenancy, gifts, termination of co-ownership, ownership arrangements, land registry procedures and legal due diligence before entering into a transaction. Real estate due diligence may include review of land registry data, possession records, encumbrances, annotations, third-party rights, construction and occupancy documentation and other circumstances relevant to the legal security of a purchaser, seller, investor or tenant. In real estate matters it is important to align contractual documentation with the actual and registered status of the property and to provide mechanisms for protection in the event of non-performance, delay, defects or dispute. The firm also advises on co-ownership relations, common parts of buildings and complex property-law situations.

Litigation and enforcement

BFMS represents clients in litigation, enforcement and other court proceedings before competent courts and authorities. Legal support includes assessment of claims, preparation of statements of claim, responses, legal remedies, enforcement proposals, objections, appeals and other submissions, as well as representation at hearings. In litigation, emphasis is placed on timely analysis of facts, evidence, procedural requirements and possible outcomes. In enforcement matters, important issues include the selection of the object and means of enforcement, assessment of collectability, the order of legal steps and the protection of creditors’ or debtors’ rights. The firm supports disputes arising from commercial, contractual, property, employment and other legal relationships where substantive arguments must be connected with procedural strategy.

Employment law

In employment law, BFMS advises employers and employees on employment contracts, work regulations, internal acts, termination of employment, dismissals, working time, salaries, employee liability, protection of dignity, discrimination and other matters arising from employment relationships. Support for employers may include drafting and aligning internal acts, preparing decisions, advising before termination and representation in employment disputes. Employees may seek advice on the legality of employer decisions, protection of employment rights and enforcement of claims before courts or other competent bodies. Timely action is particularly important in employment matters because statutory deadlines often have a decisive effect on the possibility of bringing or continuing a claim.

Contract law

BFMS drafts, reviews and negotiates contracts for individuals and legal entities, with emphasis on clear regulation of rights, obligations, deadlines, liability, security instruments and the consequences of non-performance. Legal support covers sale and purchase agreements, lease and tenancy agreements, service contracts, cooperation agreements, construction contracts, settlement agreements, amendments and other contractual documents. The purpose of contract drafting is not only to record the agreement formally, but also to anticipate possible disputed situations and reduce the risk of future misunderstanding. Particular attention is given to payment terms, deadlines, liability for damage, termination, confidentiality, warranties, jurisdiction and evidence that may be relevant if a dispute arises.

Legal support for domestic and international clients

BFMS provides legal services in Croatian, and where required by the matter, in English and German. Multilingual legal support is particularly relevant for foreign nationals, foreign companies, international business relations, real estate investments in Croatia, cross-border contracts and procedures where the Croatian legal framework must be explained to persons operating outside Croatia. The firm can prepare legal documents, summaries of legal risks, contractual documentation and correspondence adapted to the language and business context of the particular matter.
